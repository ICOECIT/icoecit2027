Venue

Patna, Bihar, India

ICoECIT 2027 will be held on 3–4 May 2027 at Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology, Patna, the host institute and organizer of the conference.

Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology, Patna

Host institute & conference venue

Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology, Patna (NSIT) is an engineering institute at Amhara, Bihta, on the western side of Patna. The conference — plenary talks and technical sessions — will be held on the NSIT campus.

As host institute and organizer of ICoECIT 2027, NSIT has faculty from its Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication and Electrical & Electronics departments serving on the conference committees.

Getting there

  • Air: Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport (PAT), Patna — approx. 35–40 km by road (about 1 hour) from the campus.
  • Rail: Bihta railway station is the nearest; Danapur and Patna Junction are the nearest major stations, with connections across India.
  • Road / Local: Bihta lies on the Patna–Buxar highway (NH-922); the campus is reachable from Patna city by app cab, taxi, bus or local train.

Explore Patna

Delegates can visit Golghar, the Bihar Museum and Patna Museum, Takht Sri Patna Sahib (Harmandir), Mahavir Mandir, the ancient site of Kumhrar and Gandhi Maidan. Nalanda and Bodh Gaya make popular day trips.
